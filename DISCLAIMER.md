# Disclaimer

**Model, not advice.** This project decomposes published gold prices and
compares them with public wage and support-price statistics. It contains no
forecasts, no price targets, and no recommendation to buy, sell, or avoid
anything. Every number links to its public source; if a source revises, we
revise.

This project is data journalism, not investment research. It shows accounting
decompositions of published prices and long-run purchasing-power ratios. It
does not predict prices and does not constitute investment advice under any
jurisdiction, including SEBI's research-analyst framework.
