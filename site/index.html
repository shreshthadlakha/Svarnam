The stack

Metal and the dollar set the price; Delhi and the market move the rest

Every column is the Mumbai ₹/10g average split into four legs: world metal price × USD/INR, customs duty, GST, and the signed residual — hatched below the baseline when it discounts.

The rupee price of gold has risen in almost every decade since FY1985-86. The gold leg dominates every year; duty, GST and the hatched residual explain the rest. Dashed rules with numbered flags mark nine policy breaks — each is explained in the list under the chart.

    Episodes

    Documented premium & discount episodes

    Dealer-quoted episodes from Reuters' "Asia Gold" reporting — restrictions and duty spikes push the local market to a premium; liberalisations and price spikes push it to a discount. Dollar-quoted figures are shown in ₹/10g at each episode month's average USD/INR.

      The sovereign stopped selling its own instrument

      India launched Sovereign Gold Bonds on 5 Nov 2015 at ₹2,684/g (2.75% interest) to shift household demand away from physical gold. Sixty-seven tranches and roughly 147 tonnes later, the last tranche issued on 21 Feb 2024 at ₹6,263/g. In Feb 2025 the government confirmed the scheme's discontinuation — with a liability near ₹1.12 lakh crore on ~132 t outstanding.

      "A rather fairly high-cost borrowing for the government… the government has chosen not to follow that path." — Economic Affairs Secretary, Feb 2025

      Source S15 (PIB/RBI notifications as reported by Moneycontrol).

      Method

      An identity, not a model

      This page decomposes a published price; it computes no fair value and makes no valuation claim. For every period:

      metal+FX leg = London USD/troy-oz ÷ 31.1034768 × 10 × USD/INR
duty leg     = day-weighted customs duty from the sourced calendar (specific ₹/10g era 1990-2011; BCD+AIDC+SWS ad-valorem era 2012→)
GST leg      = 3% on the duty-paid value, from 1 Jul 2017 (day-weighted at the boundary)
landed cost  = metal+FX + duty + GST
premium leg  = Mumbai price − landed cost   (signed — a discount renders below the baseline)

      The premium residual is displayed, never judged. Reconciliation is enforced by the build: the four legs must sum back to the published price within ±0.5% every period, and golden values are checked against the source files on every build. Annual rows use RBI's official-rate series (Table 133, regime flags included); monthly rows (2005-10 →) use the Goldhub domestic price and the Fed H.10 market rate. Excluded by design: livestock and land history (no national series exists), forward prices (we show none), state-wise layer (V2).
