## Underlying sources (each keeps its own terms)

| Source | What we use | Terms note |
|---|---|---|
| RBI Handbook of Statistics on the Indian Economy (Tables 35, 133, 164, 132, 205, 223) | Mumbai ₹/10g annual, USD/INR annual/monthly/daily | Public-sector publication; reuse with attribution; cite edition + table number |
| LBMA Gold Price PM (USD, daily) | Global free-market gold series, 1968→ | Deep history carries ICE Benchmark Administration licensing terms — this project displays **derived model outputs** (decomposition legs), not the raw licensed feed; Wayback-sourced historical copy used for research |
| WGC Goldhub averages (xlsx) | Monthly USD from 1978; monthly Indian domestic INR from Oct 2005 | World Gold Council Goldhub terms; used with attribution; not redistributed in raw form beyond the derived dataset |
| FRED DEXINUS (Fed H.10) | Daily USD/INR overlay, 1973→ | Free use with attribution to FRED |
| Labour Bureau WRRI | Rural wage rates, 20 states, 2006-07→ | Government of India publication; attribution |
| DES Agricultural Wages in India | District wage history (PDF volumes) | Government of India publication; attribution |
| CACP MSP tables & Price Policy reports | Minimum Support Prices, crop years | Government of India publication; attribution |
| Reuters "Asia Gold" dealer quotes | Documented premium/discount episodes | Cited as press reporting; episode magnitudes quoted, not reproduced as a feed |

If a source's terms change, the affected series is flagged in `site/sources.md`
and, where required, replaced with a permitted alternative.
